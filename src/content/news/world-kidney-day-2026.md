World Kidney Day is held every year on the second Thursday of March. For the rare and genetic kidney disease community it is a chance to remind decision makers that behind the CKD statistics there are more than 150 different conditions.

Our members organised local activities, from information stands in hospitals to webinars with nephrologists and geneticists.
