The European Kidney Health Alliance hosted an online session on how a European network can move rare kidney disease care from managing symptoms towards real treatment options.

FEDERG joined the discussion to bring the patient perspective. Three points stood out:

- **Diagnosis still takes too long.** Many families wait years for a genetic confirmation.
- **Registries need patients.** Research only moves when patients take part and trust how their data is used.
- **Access is uneven.** Where you live in Europe still shapes the care you receive.
