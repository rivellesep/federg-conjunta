start: 2026-10-13
location: Brussels
format: In person
organiser: European Kidney Health Alliance
summary: A forum on kidney health disparities, with a focus on the gender gap in diagnosis, treatment and transplantation.
tags: [CKD, kidney diseases]
---

Women are less likely to be referred for dialysis and transplantation in many European countries. The forum looked at the evidence and at policy options to close the gap.
