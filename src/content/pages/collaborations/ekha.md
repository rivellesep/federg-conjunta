The European Kidney Health Alliance brings together patients, health professionals and kidney foundations. FEDERG contributes the voice of people with rare and genetic kidney diseases.

Together we take part in forums, webinars and events at the European Parliament, on topics like early diagnosis, health disparities and access to transplantation.
