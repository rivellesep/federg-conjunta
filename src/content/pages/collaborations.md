FEDERG works with the key organisations and networks of the European and international kidney and rare disease community.

## Our partners

- **ERKNet**, the European Rare Kidney Disease Reference Network.
- **EKHA**, the European Kidney Health Alliance. [Read about our collaboration](/about/collaborations/ekha/).
- **EKPF**, the European Kidney Patients' Federation.
- **ERA**, the European Renal Association.
- **EURORDIS**, Rare Diseases Europe.
- **RDI**, Rare Diseases International.
