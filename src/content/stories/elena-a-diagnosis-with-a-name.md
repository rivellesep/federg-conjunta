Our son was always thirsty and tired, and he was not growing like other children. For three years we went from one specialist to another.

When a genetic test finally confirmed cystinosis, we were scared, but also relieved. At last we knew what we were fighting.

Through a parents' group we learned about specialist centres, school support and how to manage the medication routine as a family.
