I was 24 when my doctor first said the words *Alport syndrome*. I had never heard them before, and neither had anyone I knew.

## The hardest part

The hardest part was not the treatment. It was feeling alone. My friends were planning trips and careers; I was planning around dialysis sessions.

## What changed

A nurse gave me the leaflet of a patient association. At my first meeting I met people who understood without explanations. They taught me how to travel with dialysis, how to talk to my employer and how to ask my doctors better questions.

Today I volunteer with that association and help newly diagnosed patients.
