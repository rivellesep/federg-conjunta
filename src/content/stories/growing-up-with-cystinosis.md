*This is a fictional story written for a practice project. It does not describe a real person.*

Lena was diagnosed when she was eighteen months old. Her parents remember the long nights reading about a disease that affected only a few hundred children in their country.

## School, medicines and timetables

Her childhood was organised around medication every six hours. Teachers needed training, school trips needed planning, and friends had to understand why she sometimes could not join in.

## Transplant

At nineteen, Lena received a kidney from her father. The recovery was hard, but it gave her energy she had never had before.

## Finding a community

What changed everything, she says, was meeting other families through a patient association. "You stop explaining yourself. People just know."
