*This is a fictional story written for a practice project. It does not describe a real person.*

Marc had high blood pressure for years before anyone looked at his kidneys. A scan finally showed the cysts, and a genetic test confirmed the diagnosis.

## A family question

The hardest part was talking to his children. Genetic counselling helped the family understand the inheritance pattern and the options available to them.

## Taking part

Marc now takes part in a European registry. "If my data helps someone get diagnosed earlier, it is worth it."
